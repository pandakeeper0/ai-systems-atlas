# Principles & Ideas

This section collects **named, reusable ideas that people have distilled from repeated observation, theory, or practice**: principles, paradoxes, laws, effects, criteria, and similar intellectual tools.

They are smaller than a topic and more durable than a source note. The point is not to collect every technical term or definition, but to preserve ideas that give us a useful way to **reason about a problem**.

A good entry should answer four things:

> **What does it say? · Where did it come from? · What is closely related? · Why is it useful?**
